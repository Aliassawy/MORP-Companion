Calibrate

Waiting for readings.

Cover the sensor, then shine a flashlight on it. Put the lowest raw value in LIGHT_RAW_DARK and the highest in LIGHT_RAW_BRIGHT in mood_state.py.

Light only nudges MORP’s energy: dark −12, dim −5, bright +6. It never puts MORP to sleep while you’re talking.
